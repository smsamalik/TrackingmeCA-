🧑‍✈️ Driver Monitoring System (DMS)

AI That Watches the Road, So You Don't Have To Guess

AI-assisted driver monitoring identifies risky behaviour as it happens and delivers real-time alerts to help drivers self-correct and fleet managers coach proactively.

What DMS Detects

AI-Assisted Detection of Risky Driving Behaviour

Depending on the selected hardware and platform tier, TrackingmeCA's driver monitoring can identify and report:

📱

Mobile-Phone Use

Detects handheld phone use while driving.

😴

Yawning & Fatigue

Flags signs of driver drowsiness and fatigue.

👀

Distraction & Inattention

Identifies distracted or inattentive driving patterns.

🚬

Smoking

Detects smoking behaviour behind the wheel.

🔗

Seat Belt Compliance

Flags failure to wear a seat belt.

🛣️

Eyes Off Road

Detects when a driver's eyes leave the road for extended periods.

🚫

Camera Obstruction

Alerts when the driver-facing camera is blocked or covered.

⚡

Harsh Events & Speeding

Flags harsh braking, acceleration, cornering and speeding.

Real-Time Alerts

Alerts Where They Matter, When They Matter

Depending on the selected hardware and platform, alerts can be delivered:

  • To the driver in-cab, as an immediate in-vehicle audio or visual alert
  • To the fleet manager or dispatcher dashboard, in real time
  • As part of a driver safety-scoring and coaching report

Available alert types, sensitivity and delivery channels vary by camera hardware, firmware and the platform tier selected.
