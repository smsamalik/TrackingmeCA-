B.C. Commercial Dashcam Readiness

B.C. has passed legislation requiring dashcams on heavy commercial vehicles. Here's what's confirmed so far, and how TrackingMe.ca can help you prepare.

🍁

What's Confirmed

  • B.C.'s Bill M217, the Dashboard Cameras in Commercial Vehicles Act, passed third reading in the B.C. Legislature on May 25, 2026, with all-party support.
  • As reported, it applies to commercial vehicles with a GVWR or registered gross weight exceeding 11,793 kg (approx. 26,000 lb) — generally semis and heavy straight trucks.
  • The reported requirement is for a forward-facing camera only — driver-facing/in-cab cameras are not mandated by this specific bill.
  • It is expected to take effect approximately six months after royal assent, which would place compliance in late 2026 or early 2027 based on public reporting available at the time of writing.
⚠️

Still To Be Finalized

  • Exact royal assent date and final in-force compliance deadline
  • Final published regulations covering technical specifications (e.g., resolution, night vision, retention period)
  • Enforcement mechanisms and penalties
  • Any exemptions by vehicle class or operation type
Legal disclaimer: This information reflects publicly reported details about British Columbia's dashcam legislation for heavy commercial vehicles as of the time of writing and is provided for general awareness only — it is not legal advice. Final hardware configuration, technical specifications and compliance deadlines depend on the applicable vehicle type, operation, and the finalized provincial regulations. Confirm current requirements directly with the Government of British Columbia or a qualified legal/compliance advisor before making purchasing or compliance decisions.

Privacy & Consent

Responsible Video & Audio Collection

In-cab video (and, where enabled, audio) involves personal information about drivers and, at times, third parties. TrackingMe.ca supports fleets in meeting their privacy obligations, including PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy law.

📄

Notice & Consent

Fleets are responsible for providing drivers with clear notice of in-cab recording and obtaining any consent required under applicable law before activation.

🔒

Retention & Access

Video is retained and access-controlled according to your configured policy; retention periods and access permissions are set per fleet.

Security
