🍁 About TrackingmeCA

Smarter Fleet Technology. Canadian Support. Competitive Pricing.

TrackingmeCA exists to give Canadian fleets — from single-truck owner-operators to national enterprises — access to reliable, compliance-focused telematics without the cost and complexity of the largest providers.

Our Mission

A Canadian Alternative, Built for Canadian Roads

Canadian fleets operate in unique conditions — long distances, harsh winters, cross-border routes and provincial regulatory variation. TrackingmeCA was built around that reality: quality hardware, Canadian-based support, and pricing that works for fleets of every size.

We work with owner-operators, regional carriers, cold-chain distributors, municipal fleets and everything in between — matching the right combination of ELD, video telematics, GPS tracking, fuel monitoring, CAN Bus data and cold-chain monitoring to each operation's needs.

Coast to coast — a growing installer and support network across Canada.
