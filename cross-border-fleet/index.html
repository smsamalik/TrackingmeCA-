🛂 Cross-Border Fleet Technology

Fleet Technology for Canada–U.S. Operations

Fleets running between Canada and the U.S. deal with two sets of rules, two sets of expectations, and one truck. Here's what to confirm before you rely on any system operationally.

Discuss Cross-Border Fleet Requirements
What's Different

What Cross-Border Fleets Need to Think About

📋

Different HOS Requirements

Canadian and U.S. Hours of Service rules aren't identical — confirm how your ELD configuration handles both jurisdictions.

🛂

Border Operations

Border crossings add wait time and documentation steps that affect route and HOS planning.

📍

Vehicle Visibility

GPS coverage should hold up on both sides of the border, not just domestically.

📝

Driver Logs

Electronic logs need to reflect the correct ruleset for wherever the driver currently is.

🧾

IFTA

Cross-border mileage still needs to be tracked accurately for fuel-tax reporting.

🎥

Incident Evidence

Video evidence matters regardless of which side of the border an incident happens on.

Confirm before you rely on this operationally: Cross-border HOS and ELD requirements can differ by jurisdiction and change over time. This page describes general considerations, not a guarantee of specific regulatory functionality. Confirm current cross-border requirements for your specific configuration with a qualified compliance advisor and the applicable regulator before relying on any system for cross-border compliance.
